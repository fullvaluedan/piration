# Store listing draft — Piration

**Title:** Piration  
**Short:** Offline pirate RPG — quests, cards, ships.  
**Full:**  
Sail offline in Piration, an archive-inspired pirate RPG. Send your crew on quests for XP and crafting materials, build ships from Skiff to Galleon, and fight gauntlet battles with a growing card deck. No account required. Progress saves on your device.

Inspired by open-sourced Pirate Nation materials (CC0 art names / community port). Not affiliated with Proof of Play.
