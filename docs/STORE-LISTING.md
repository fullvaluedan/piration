# Store listing draft — Piration

**Title:** Piration  
**Short:** Offline pirate RPG — encounters, captains, endless mode.
**Full:**
Sail offline in Piration, an archive-inspired pirate RPG. Voyage into random
monster encounters, collect loot and cards, craft ships from Skiff to
Dreadnought, and unlock six captains with unique decks and abilities — then
chase your high score in endless waves. No energy, no account, no ads.
Progress saves on your device.

Inspired by open-sourced Pirate Nation materials (CC0 art names / community port). Not affiliated with Proof of Play.
